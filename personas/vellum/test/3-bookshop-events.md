# Bookshop events

A website for an independent bookshop in a mid-sized city. The shop runs about eight events a
month: author readings, a book club and children's story hours.

- People find the site from social media posts and local listings, mostly on their phones.
- They want to know what is on, when, whether it costs money, and how to get a seat.
- Some events sell out. Others have free entry with no booking.
- The owners want the site to feel like the shop: warm and a little eccentric.
- The owners update it themselves, so it must not depend on custom photography for each event.

Screens to design: the home page, and the page for one event.
