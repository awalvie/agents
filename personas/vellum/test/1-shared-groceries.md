# Shared grocery list

A mobile app for a household of two to five people who shop for one home.

- Anyone in the household adds items during the week, often in a few seconds while cooking.
- One person shops, usually in a busy store with one hand on a trolley.
- The shopper must see what is left to buy, and must not buy the same item twice when two
  people shop at the same time.
- People want to know who asked for an item when it is unclear ("which cheese?").
- Most users are not interested in technology. Some are older parents.

Screens to design: the list, and adding an item.
