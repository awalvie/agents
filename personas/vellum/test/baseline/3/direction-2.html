On an island that is sinking an inch a year, the Hale family keep planting apple trees. Mara Lindqvist's second novel is about stubbornness, salt water, and the price of staying put.

She will read the orchard chapter, then answer questions from Ruth and from you. Signed copies will be on the table by the till.

Ruth says: “I cried on the number 9 bus. Bring a handkerchief.”
