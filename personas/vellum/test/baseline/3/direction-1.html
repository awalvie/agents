About the book

Mara Lindqvist's second novel follows three generations of the Hale family as the sea creeps up their orchard, one row of trees at a time.

She'll read for about twenty minutes, then Ruth will ask her the questions you are too polite to ask. Signed copies on the table by the till.

Good to know

Tea and apple cake are free. Wine is £3 a glass. The shop cat, Borges, may attend. He has not read the book.
