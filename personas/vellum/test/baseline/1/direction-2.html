Direction 2: Home mode and Shop mode

The app has two faces. At home it opens on the add box, so adding takes one tap and a word. In the store the shopper presses "Start shopping" and gets a dark, high-contrast list with huge cards and all controls at the bottom, under the thumb. When two people shop, the app splits the list so they never pick the same thing. Tap a card in the middle phone to put it in the trolley.
