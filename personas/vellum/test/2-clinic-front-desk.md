# Clinic front desk

A web app for the front desk of a physiotherapy clinic with six therapists and about 90
appointments a day.

- One or two receptionists use it all day on a desktop screen, often while on the phone.
- They book, move and cancel appointments, check patients in, and take payments.
- Therapists work different hours, and some treatments need a specific room or machine.
- Late patients and last-minute cancellations happen every day. The desk must fill gaps quickly.
- Receptionists know the tool well after the first week. Speed matters more than first-time ease.

Screens to design: the day view of the schedule, and booking an appointment.
