Direction 3: the list on the fridge, where everyone writes in their own ink

Most households already share a paper list, so the app keeps its habits: lines in the order people wrote them, each person's name in the margin and their own ink colour, and things crossed out rather than ticked. "Who asked?" is answered on every line without a tap. Tap a line on the first screen to cross it out. The second screen shows what tapping a name in the margin opens; that tap is not wired on the first screen. Type "cheese" on the third.
