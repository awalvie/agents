All sources were read on 2026-09-26. "Full" means I read the full text. "Partial" means I read the relevant sections. "Abstract" means I read the abstract or a summary only. "Quoted" means I saw the claim only as quoted in another source.

Count by type (59 in total):

## Research papers

6. Doshi, A. R., Hauser, O. P. (2024). "Generative AI enhances individual creativity but reduces the collective diversity of novel content." 7. Anderson, B. R., Shah, J. H., Kreminski, M. (2024). "Homogenization Effects of Large Language Models on Human Creative Ideation." Creativity & Cognition '24. https://mkremins.github.io/publications/Homogenization_C&C2024.pdf. Abstract and first page.

Australasian Marketing Journal 15(2), 42–54. https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1441358207700423. Abstract via search, plus Sharp's blog summary (listed under journalism).
